# Privacy Policy

Nudge is designed to be privacy-first.

## Data collected

- Nudge does **not** collect or transmit personal data to any server.

## Data stored on device

- Your selected apps and their weights are stored **locally on your device** to make the app work.

## Permissions

- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: used to show a persistent notification to open Settings quickly.\n+- **INTERNET**: used by React Native/Expo runtime (no backend calls are required by Nudge itself).\n+- Other permissions may appear depending on device/Android version; only those required for the app’s function should be granted.

## Contact

If you have questions, open an issue in this repository.
